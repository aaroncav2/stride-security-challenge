Privacy Policy

STRIDE Security Challenge (the "app")
Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

The app is an offline educational card game. It has no accounts, does not ask for personal information, and does not send your gameplay to us or to any server. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your current game state and progress are saved locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app displays banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use the Android advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and (where permitted) personalize ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for a child audience.
